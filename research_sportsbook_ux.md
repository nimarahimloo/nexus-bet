## Findings from the bet365 listing

The listing explicitly highlights live in-game betting, live streaming, Match Live with real-time stats, sports statistics, personalized display, search across sport/league/event/market, news and betting insights, Same Game Parlay, boosts, payments, and responsible gaming controls such as time-outs, deposit limits, and self-exclusion. The product pattern to carry forward is not a single crowded dashboard; it is a set of fast, task-oriented entry points: discover, live, search, build a slip, monitor a bet, and manage control settings.

## Findings from sportsbook UX research

The mobile-first direction should prioritize personalization, fast access to frequent sports, clear market grouping, large touch targets, sticky or docked bet-slip access, and low-friction confirmation. Live surfaces should expose the match state and the key action without requiring the user to scan a desktop-style table. Responsible-gaming controls should be visible in the account area instead of buried in legal copy.

## Nexus Bet implications

Nexus Bet currently has the visual shell but needs a more complete product IA: a mobile bottom navigation with Home, Live, Search, Bets, and Wallet; a dedicated live-center pattern; collapsible market groups; a persistent compact slip dock; bet history and open-bet tracking; search; favorites; and clearer transaction/account flows. Copy should be short and operational in Persian, for example: «زنده»، «بازارها»، «بلیت من»، «جست‌وجوی مسابقه»، «موجودی قابل‌استفاده»، «بررسی بلیت» and «تنظیم محدودیت». The product should present examples and sample states as preview data until live sports and settlement providers are connected; it should not imply that sample odds or rewards are live or guaranteed.
